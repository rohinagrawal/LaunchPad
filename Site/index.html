Illustration of the Jobs board with made-up companies. The live tracker is below.

Built around three promises.

01

It is yours.

The app is plain Python and HTML with nothing to install. Your data is two JSON files in your own repository, not rows in someone else's database.

02

It works without AI.

The tracker is a complete app by itself. The skills add searching, outreach drafts, form filling and interview prep when you open the folder in Claude Code.

03

Nothing is sent without your yes.

Connection requests, messages, emails and applications all wait for your approval. Autopilot exists, and it is off until you switch it on.

Referrals as a pipeline

Found, queued, requested, connected, asked. You always know who is waiting on whom.

Insights that stay honest

Questions people ask.

Is it free?

Yes. LaunchPad is open source under the MIT licence. The skills run in Claude Code, which you bring yourself.

Do I need Claude to use it?

No. The tracker is a complete app by itself. The skills add searching, outreach drafts, form filling and interview prep.

Where does my data live?

In two JSON files, Data/data.json and Data/contacts.json, inside your own copy of the repository. Keep that copy private.

Will it message people or apply for me on its own?

Not unless you say so. Each message and application waits for your approval. Autopilot is opt-in and you choose its rules.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, if you host a copy. The hosting guide puts the tracker online behind a password.

Can it run every day by itself?

Yes. The automation guide sets up a daily routine for the search, the people research and the drafts.
