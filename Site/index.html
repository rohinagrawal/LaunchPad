Built around three promises.

01

It is yours.

The app is plain Python and HTML with nothing to install. Your data is two JSON files in your own repository, not rows in someone else's database.

02

AI does the legwork.

Finding roles and referrers, drafting each ask, sending what you approved and following up all run as skills your AI agent follows. The tracker still works by hand without it, but the automation is the point.

03

Nothing is sent without your yes.

Connection requests, messages, emails and applications all wait for your approval. Autopilot exists, and it is off until you switch it on.

Running in about a minute.

You need Python 3.9 or later. There are no dependencies and there is no build step.

  1. 1

    Make your own private copy

    Your job search data is committed to your copy, so keep it private.

  2. 2

    Start the tracker

    Then open http://localhost:8787.

Questions people ask.

Is it free?

Yes. LaunchPad is open source under the MIT licence. The skills run in an AI agent that you bring yourself.

Do I need an AI agent to use it?

For the automation, yes. Searching for roles, finding referrers, drafting and sending outreach, filling forms and interview prep are skills an AI agent runs for you. Without one you still have the whole tracker, filled in by hand.

Which AI agents does it work with?

The skills are plain-text playbooks in the repository, so any agent that reads a project folder can follow them. The steps that act for you, such as searching LinkedIn, sending outreach and filling forms, need an agent that can drive a browser and read your email. The guides walk through the setup with Claude Code.

Where does my data live?

In two JSON files, Data/data.json and Data/contacts.json, inside your own copy of the repository. Keep that copy private.

Will it message people or apply for me on its own?

Not unless you say so. Each message and application waits for your approval. Autopilot is opt-in and you choose its rules.

How is this different from a hosted AI job-agent service?

LaunchPad runs on your own computer, costs nothing and is open source, so you can read and change all of it. There is no account, and your applications and contacts never sit on someone else's servers. The trade-off is that you set it up yourself and bring your own AI agent for the automation.

Does it apply to jobs automatically?

It fills the application form from your answer bank and resume, then stops. You read it and press Submit yourself, and the card moves to Applied.

Does it work with LinkedIn?

Yes. One skill searches LinkedIn Jobs in your own logged-in browser, another finds people who could refer you and sends the connection requests you approved, within the daily and weekly limits you set. Referral asks can go by LinkedIn message or by email.

What do the AI skills cost?

LaunchPad itself is free. The skills run in your own AI agent, on whatever plan you already have for it. The tracker by itself needs none.

Does it run on Windows and Linux?

The tracker is plain Python and a web page, so it should run anywhere Python 3.9 does. It is developed and tested on macOS.

How do I get updates?

Keep your private copy pointed at the public repository as an upstream and pull from it. The setup guide shows how.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, if you host a copy. The hosting guide puts the tracker online behind a password.

Can it run every day by itself?

Yes. The automation guide sets up a daily routine for the search, the people research and the drafts.
