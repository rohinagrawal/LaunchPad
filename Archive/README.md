# Archive

Everything here was moved out of the working folders by
`LaunchPad App/cleanup.py` (rules in `Referral/Referral-Playbook.md`,
section 6). Nothing is deleted. Git history also keeps every version.

- `tracker/jobs-<year>.json`: job cards archived 30 days after closing. To
  restore one, copy its object back into `Data/data.json`.
- `tracker/people-<year>.json`: Declined, No response and Skipped people
  archived 30 days after closing.
- `listings/<YYYY-MM>/`: job descriptions of archived cards, and daily
  `New-Openings` reviews older than 14 days.
- `applications/<Company>/`: referral notes and documents for companies with
  no open roles left.
